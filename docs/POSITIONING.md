# Positioning (read before writing the pitch or the README)

A competitor with a very similar idea introduced itself in the Buildathon group: Sivan Ai
(sivantech.online). Everything below about Sivan comes from its public website and is its own marketing,
so verify before saying anything about it in public.

## What Sivan says it has
- Chat-first: agreements created and managed in WhatsApp, Telegram or a web app.
- Milestone-protected service agreements, release timers for client ghosting, instant dispute freeze,
  two-way evidence upload.
- Bank off-ramps to local accounts, plus phone-number payments for people without a wallet.
- Seven chains, Stellar being one of them. Describes itself as non-custodial.

## What that means for us
We cannot beat it on distribution, cash-out or breadth in one week, and we should not try. Milestones, release
timers and dispute freezes are no longer our novelty. Do not say nobody does that.

## What we can credibly say is different (check each against Sivan before using it)
1. **Rules enforced by a public Soroban contract.** Three different keys for client, freelancer and arbiter.
   The arbiter can only choose how one disputed milestone is split. The arbiter fee is fixed. Every path
   has a way out: client silent, arbiter silent, freelancer silent, freelancer never accepts. Funds are never stuck.
2. **Trust tiers.** A new freelancer is not stuck at zero. Tiers shown to clients and set the review window.
   Nothing limits what a freelancer can earn. Outside history can be proven and counted.
3. **Evidence-based dispute handling.** An agent reads both sides and recommends a ruling with reasons and
   cited evidence. A human decides. The ruling and the evidence fingerprints are on the ledger.
4. **Depth on Stellar.** Soroban contract, test USDC, events, one chain done well.

## Pitch lines
- Lead with trust and fair disputes, not with milestone escrow.
- Position as the trust and dispute engine that chat apps and marketplaces could plug into.
- Be honest about what is simulated (verification, cash-out). Show the dispute flow live. It is the strongest part.

## Demo order
1. New freelancer with a longer review window, trusted freelancer with a shorter one.
2. Normal approval and release.
3. Client silent, auto-release.
4. Dispute: both sides add evidence, agent recommends, human rules, contract pays out.
5. Arbiter silent: default split. Freelancer silent: client reclaims.
